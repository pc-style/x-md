Reading posts

    Public accounts only, no login. Posts are read through the x.md API on this demo's server; nothing is saved.

    How it scores

    • Two streams at once. The account's own posts and replies, and posts by others that mention it.
    • Each post becomes interactions. A reply is worth 3, a quote 2.5, a mention 1.5 and a repost 1, halving every 30 days.
    • Both ways counts double. Given and received combine as (√given + √received)², so mutual conversations move closest.
