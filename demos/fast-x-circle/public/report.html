How it is built

Fast X Circle keeps X Circle’s scoring, rings, and PNG export, and fills the picture while x.md is still collecting the account’s posts and incoming mentions.

Same ranking

A reply is worth 3, a quote 2.5, a mention 1.5, and a repost 1. Each value halves every 30 days. The two directions combine as the square of the sum of their square roots: one way stays the plain sum, and an even exchange doubles it. The reply chain at the start of an answer is not a mention. Up to 50 people are drawn, largest nearest the centre.

What changed

X Circle walks the public timeline and the mention search one page after another, then draws. This demo asks x.md for two things at once. A bulk import reads the last 7 days of posts, replies, and reposts (the part of the window that weighs most, and the part a single import can finish quickly). In parallel, search reads the full 120 days: the account’s own posts in four time slices, reposts in two slices, and incoming @handle posts in four slices. The canvas updates as soon as either source finds a person. Portrait URLs that the posts do not already carry are read from the public profile endpoint X Circle uses, because x.md’s profile route also downloads a page of posts.
